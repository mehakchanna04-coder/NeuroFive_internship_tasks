# Happy Pie Bakery — Instagram Campaign

A complete, on-brand Instagram campaign for **Happy Pie Bakery**, a fictional
neighborhood pie shop. 12 assets total: 5 feed posts, 2 Stories, and one
5-slide promotional carousel — sharing one consistent visual identity.

## Brand system

| Element | Choice |
|---|---|
| Logo | Pie icon (crimped crust + lattice) + "Happy Pie" wordmark + tracked "BAKERY" label |
| Palette | Cream `#FFF7EC` background · Crust tan `#E7B978` · Brown `#5C3A21` (text) · Terracotta `#D97C4F` (CTA/accent) · Berry `#B5433F` (secondary accent) |
| Typography | **GFS Baskerville** (serif) for headlines — warm, editorial, bakery-appropriate. **Poppins** (sans) for body copy, labels, and buttons — clean and highly legible at small sizes. |
| Recurring motifs | Scallop "pie-crust" border strip, the pie icon (recolored per post to suggest different fillings), consistent rounded-pill CTA buttons, consistent footer (`@happypiebakery · 142 Maple Street · Open 7am–7pm Daily`) |
| Voice | Warm, a little playful, never salesy-loud except on the promo slide (which intentionally breaks the palette to a bold terracotta background for contrast) |

## Notes on the design system

- Every asset uses the **same** logo lockup, font pairing, color palette,
  footer, and CTA button shape — the goal was that any single asset should
  be instantly recognizable as "Happy Pie" even without the logo visible.
- The pie icon is reused everywhere but **recolored per post** (berry red,
  caramel, cream, etc.) to hint at different flavors without needing real
  product photography.
- Post 5 and Story 2 deliberately invert the palette (terracotta/dark
  background instead of cream) — reserved *only* for promotions/urgency, so
  that inversion itself signals "special offer" to a repeat viewer.
